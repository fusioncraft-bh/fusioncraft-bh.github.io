Our story

A family recipe, a full table, a warm welcome.

Zaytoun began with a simple idea: bring the flavours of the Levant to Manama, and serve them the way our grandmothers did — generously.

Every dish starts with ingredients from the local market that morning, and ends at a table meant to be lingered over.

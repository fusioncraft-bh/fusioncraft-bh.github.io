FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does it take?
Most sites are live within about 5 days. Bigger, custom projects take a little longer — we'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
Do I need to buy hosting or a domain?
No. We can host your site for free, so there's nothing to pay for hosting. A custom domain (like yourshop.com) is optional and usually only around BHD 3–5 per year if you want one.
What's included?
Design, a mobile-first build, setting up your content, a WhatsApp and contact button, Google Maps, and launching it live. Everything you need to be found and look professional.
Can I update it later?
Absolutely. Send us your changes on WhatsApp and we'll handle them. For businesses that update often, we offer an optional low-cost care plan.
How do I get started?
Just message us on WhatsApp. We'll have a quick chat about your business, agree on a package, and get building — no long forms or meetings.

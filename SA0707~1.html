About us

Two decades of woodworking

Oud Woodworks started as a small family workshop in Bahrain and grew through one thing: honest craftsmanship. Two decades on, we still build every piece by hand.

From a single dining table to a full kitchen fit-out, we treat every project as our own — choosing the right wood, perfecting the joinery, and finishing it to last a lifetime.
